Trace

Kids pick an animal, trace its dotted outline with an Apple Pencil and colour it in. I built the first version in a morning, and it has grown since.

Try it at kids.sauravsood.com ↗
Chapter 1

The smudge

  • Kids love drawing on an iPad, but most drawing apps are made for adults, with rows of buttons and menus that open by accident.

  • A palm resting on the glass leaves a stray mark every time it touches.

  • I wanted an app a child could use alone, with big pictures, big colours and nothing to read.

Chapter 2

One morning

  • I asked Claude to see what already exists on GitHub. It picked perfect-freehand, a small library that turns Pencil pressure into strokes that look hand-drawn.

  • The rest is plain HTML with no build step. The dotted animal sits under a see-through drawing layer, so scribbles never cover the dots.

  • It took about an hour from my first question to the app going live. I made the calls, and Claude wrote the code and drew the animals.

Chapter 3

What kids practise

  • A child holds the Pencil the same way they’ll later hold a pencil to write.

  • Following dots makes them slow down, steer and stop on purpose.

  • The curves, loops and straight lines in the animals are the same strokes that later turn into letters.

  • There are eleven colours to name and pick, and yes, the lion can be blue.

  • There are no ads, timers or scores, and nothing to buy.

Chapter 4

What came later

  • The first eight animals are now 32, sorted into land, water, farm, sky and dinosaurs.

  • With My Animals, a kid photographs an animal at home, or a toy, and the iPad turns the photo into a dotted outline to trace.

  • When a drawing is finished, Animate now puts it in an old nursery-rhyme picture book, and the pages curl as you turn them.

  • A green play button reads each page aloud and turns to the next one.

The grown-up details

  • Once the Pencil touches the screen, the app ignores hands for a minute.

  • Several kids can share one iPad, each with their own drawings. Adding a kid needs a grown-up.

  • Drawings are saved, and the app keeps working offline.
