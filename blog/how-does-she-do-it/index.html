I don't know how she does it

A thank-you to my wife, written by someone who gets a full night's sleep and still can't keep up.

I genuinely don't know how she does it. We're both 36 now. Our baby is six months old, which means my wife hasn't really slept in about six months. I've tried to keep up. I can't.

It started with the surgery. There was no gap after it, no stretch of recovery of any kind, just straight into sleepless nights and feeds around the clock. And somehow, through all of that, she's still managing everything with a smile.

And that's not even the whole list. She's finding time for our elder one, now four, with patience I didn't know a person could have on that little sleep. She's on top of the dogs, right down to their dental care. She's figuring out what we eat, keeping it interesting and healthy, and routing new dishes to our cook. The house still looks the way she wants it to look.

On top of that she's getting fitter after a second C-section, and she's excelling at work, so much so that she's getting rewards while she's on maternity leave.
